Coffee is ordered by sweetness here, not by size.

In a Cairo ahwa you don't ask for a small or a large. You ask for how much sugar goes into the pot before the water boils. Three tins, three answers. Tap one to read what's inside.

How to brew it

Cold water and coffee go into the kanaka together, sugar with them. Heat slowly until the foam climbs to the rim, lift it off, let it settle, return it once. Pour without stirring, and leave the last centimetre in the cup.

Roasted in Cairo. Packed and shipped from Budapest, usually within two days.
